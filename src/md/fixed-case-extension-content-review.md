# Fixed-case extension and mastery review packet

Original exercises drafted for Release 4. All 288 new focused pairs remain **pending independent clinical and native-language review**. Their 96 mastery scenes reuse exact source wording; bridges and prompts are original. This packet supports review, not a declaration of clinical validity.

The nine original cases retain their fixed level and existing history. This batch completes the remaining eight; Sara’s earlier extension banks and mastery path are unchanged. No new diagnosis or historical event is added to a case dossier. Session events in exercise statements are simulated practice moments.

The extension framework is informed by [EFT competence sections 3.1–3.4](https://emotionfocusedtherapy.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/FC-EFT-Competence-v1.03-2.pdf). Original examples are not quotations or endorsed treatment protocols.

Editorial boundaries also draw on [NICE guidance on transitions and endings](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg78/chapter/Recommendations), [PTSD guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng116/chapter/Recommendations) and [self-harm care](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng225/chapter/recommendations). In these new paths, Aisha and Marcus have non-acute practice moments. The selected material does not simulate managing an immediate crisis. Bounded endings must not be used as substitutes for assessment, care planning or specialist treatment. No claim of efficacy for the app follows from these sources.

Review priorities: respect the client’s choice of direction; distinguish hurt from permission to intimidate; avoid requiring trauma details, emotional intensity or verbal performance; notice a small change without promising it lasts; end with unfinished work and actual contact arrangements.
